# Research Workflow

1. State the decision the research must support.
2. Separate known facts, assumptions, and questions.
3. Define source quality and freshness requirements.
4. Gather multiple primary or authoritative sources when internet access is
   available.
5. Record evidence, date, scope, limitations, and conflicting findings.
6. Convert findings into product options with user impact, cost, risk, and
   recommendation.
7. Store durable project research under `docs/research/`.

Research does not authorize implementation or external purchases.
